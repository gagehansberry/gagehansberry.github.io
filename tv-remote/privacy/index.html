Telly Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Telly does not collect any data. It is a remote control for your TV, and it only talks to devices on your own home network. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing it handles is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What Telly connects to

Telly asks for permission to use your local network, because that is how it reaches your TV. It connects to:

  • Your TV. The first time you connect, your TV shows a prompt asking whether to allow Telly. Nothing is controlled until you accept it on the TV, and you can remove the permission from the TV later. Telly sends the buttons you press and the text you type, and receives what it needs to show the remote, such as the volume, the current app and the icons of the TV's apps. When the TV supports it, the connection is encrypted and Telly checks that it is talking to the same TV it paired with. If your TV doesn't support an encrypted connection, Telly uses the TV's unencrypted one, which stays on your home network.
  • Turning the TV on. To switch the TV on, Telly sends a standard "wake" signal to the TV's address on your home network.
  • A Sonos speaker, if you set one up. Telly can adjust a Sonos speaker's sound settings over your home network. Sonos speakers don't offer an encrypted connection for this, so these requests are unencrypted, and Telly only sends them to an address on your home network.

Telly does not connect to the internet, and none of this traffic leaves your home network.

What Telly keeps on your iPhone

To work, Telly stores a few things in the app's own private storage on your iPhone:

  • Your TVs: each TV's name, its address on your home network, the hardware address used to turn it on, the apps you pin, and when you last connected.
  • The pairing key your TV issued, and a fingerprint of the TV's certificate. These are kept in the iPhone's Keychain, can only be read while your iPhone is unlocked, and are never copied to other devices or backups.
  • Icons of your TV's apps, copied from the TV so the remote can show them.
  • Your Sonos speaker's name and address on your home network, if you set one up.
  • Things you create in Telly: macros and saved picture settings.
  • Your settings.

Apart from the Keychain items, these are protected by iOS data protection, which encrypts them using your device passcode.

Typing on the TV. When the TV asks for text, such as a search, what you type in Telly goes straight to the TV. Telly does not save it, and it cannot read anything that was already in the field.

Siri and Shortcuts

Telly offers Siri and Shortcuts actions, such as turning the TV off or changing its volume. Siri handles your request under Apple's privacy policy, and Telly carries it out on your iPhone by talking to your TV. Your iPhone has to be unlocked for these actions to reach your TV.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Telly sends no data to the developer or to any third party, and contains no third-party code or services.

If you are testing Telly through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That comes from Apple, not from the app.

Deleting your data

  • Forgetting a TV in Telly removes its details, its pairing key and its cached app icons from your iPhone. To remove Telly's permission from the TV as well, use the TV's own settings.
  • Deleting the app removes what Telly keeps in its own storage. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so forget your TVs in Telly first if you want the pairing keys removed too. Removing Telly's permission on the TV makes any leftover key useless.
  • If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Telly's settings and stored files in that backup, under your device's backup settings and Apple's terms. The pairing keys are never included.

Children

Telly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Telly ever begins sending data anywhere other than your own devices, this policy will say exactly what, to whom and why before that version of the app is released.

Contact

Telly is in testing. Questions about this policy can be sent through TestFlight: open the TestFlight app, choose Telly, and tap Send Beta Feedback. Before Telly is released on the App Store, an email address will be added here.
