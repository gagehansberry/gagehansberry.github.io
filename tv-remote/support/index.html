Clicker Support

A remote control for webOS TVs that works over your home Wi‑Fi.

Getting help

Email dev.gagehansberry@protonmail.com. Say what you tapped and what you saw, and include your TV's model if you know it. A screenshot helps.

Testing through TestFlight? You can also open the TestFlight app, choose Clicker and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Clicker and choose "Share Beta Feedback".

Common questions

What do I need?
An iPhone and a TV running webOS, both on the same Wi‑Fi network. Clicker has been tested on a 2026 OLED TV running webOS 26. Other webOS TVs should work, but they haven't been tested yet. If yours doesn't, please tell us its model and what happened.

What is Clicker Pro?
Connecting to your TV is Clicker Pro: a one-time purchase, shared with your Family Sharing group, with a free 14-day trial (Settings → Clicker Pro, on the Connect screen). The demo works without it. Your saved TVs stay if the trial ends, and connect again the moment you buy.

Will Clicker work with my TV?
Use the free trial to find out before you buy. The trial costs nothing and never turns into a charge: when it ends, nothing is billed, and Clicker Pro is only bought if you choose it. If Clicker can't connect to your TV, the questions below may help, or email us with your TV's model. If you bought Clicker Pro and it doesn't work with your TV, you can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Clicker Pro, but it shows as locked.
Open Clicker Pro (on the Connect screen, or under Advanced) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I try Clicker without a TV?
Yes. On the Connect screen, scroll down and tap Try the Demo. It opens a pretend TV so you can try the remote, the Apps tab, typing and the settings. A banner shows the whole time you're in the demo. It doesn't connect to anything, and the pretend TV is never added to your TVs. Tap Disconnect to leave.

My TV isn't listed.
Clicker finds webOS TVs on your Wi‑Fi network whether or not they have AirPlay, and lists AirPlay devices as well. Searching the whole network can take about ten seconds. A TV found without AirPlay is listed as "webOS TV" with its address underneath. If your TV still isn't listed, make sure it's switched on, that your iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi‑Fi network, and that Clicker is allowed to use the local network (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network). You can also enter the TV's IP address in Clicker. You'll usually find it in the TV's network settings.

How do I pair?
Choose your TV in Clicker, then accept the prompt that appears on the TV. You only need to do this once for each TV.

Clicker can't turn my TV on.
A TV that's switched off can only be woken over the network if it allows that. Look in the TV's settings for an option to turn it on over Wi‑Fi or from a mobile device, and turn it on. Clicker also needs to have connected to the TV at least once while it was on.

Is a Sonos speaker required?
No. If you have a Sonos speaker, Clicker can adjust its sound settings, but everything else works without one.

How do I remove a TV?
Forget it in Clicker. To remove Clicker's permission on the TV too, use the TV's own settings. See the privacy policy for what Clicker stores and how to delete it.
