Telly Support

A remote control for webOS TVs that works over your home Wi‑Fi.

Getting help

Telly is in testing. Send feedback through TestFlight: open the TestFlight app, choose Telly and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Telly and choose "Share Beta Feedback". Say what you tapped and what you saw, and include your TV's model if you know it. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

What do I need?
An iPhone and a TV running webOS, both on the same Wi‑Fi network. Telly has been tested on a 2026 OLED TV running webOS 26. Other webOS TVs should work, but they haven't been tested yet. If yours doesn't, please tell us its model and what happened.

My TV isn't listed.
Telly finds TVs that have AirPlay turned on. Turn AirPlay on in the TV's settings, or enter the TV's IP address in Telly. You'll usually find the IP address in the TV's network settings. Also check that your iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi‑Fi network, and that Telly is allowed to use the local network (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network).

How do I pair?
Choose your TV in Telly, then accept the prompt that appears on the TV. You only need to do this once for each TV.

Telly can't turn my TV on.
A TV that's switched off can only be woken over the network if it allows that. Look in the TV's settings for an option to turn it on over Wi‑Fi or from a mobile device, and turn it on. Telly also needs to have connected to the TV at least once while it was on.

Is a Sonos speaker required?
No. If you have a Sonos speaker, Telly can adjust its sound settings, but everything else works without one.

How do I remove a TV?
Forget it in Telly. To remove Telly's permission on the TV too, use the TV's own settings. See the privacy policy for what Telly stores and how to delete it.
