Sanitize Support

Removes tracking from links and hidden details from photos, on your iPhone.

Getting help

Sanitize is in testing. Send feedback through TestFlight: open the TestFlight app, choose Sanitize and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Sanitize and choose "Share Beta Feedback". Say what you shared or tapped and what you saw. If a link was cleaned wrongly, include the link if you're comfortable sharing it.

Common questions

How do I clean a link?
In the app you're reading, tap Share, then Sanitize, then tap to copy the clean link. If Sanitize isn't in the list, scroll to the end, tap More (or Edit Actions) and turn it on. You can also paste a link into the Links tab, or use the "Sanitize URL" action in Shortcuts.

How do I clean a photo?
Share the photo to Sanitize, or choose it in the Photos tab. Sanitize makes a clean copy without the location, camera, date and other hidden details, and tells you what it removed. Save the clean copy to Photos or share it straight from the Photos tab. Your original photo isn't changed.

What does Check Photo do?
Before you share a screenshot or photo, Check Photo looks for things that could reveal who you are or where you live: a house number, a street name, a license plate, a name on a letter, an email address, a phone number, a card number. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it also understands the text, so it can spot a name or a school. Everything happens on your iPhone. Each finding has a switch to cover it; drag over anything else; turn on Cover faces if you want faces hidden too. Covered areas are filled in solid in a new copy, so unlike a scribble or a blur, what was underneath can't be recovered. It can miss things, so look the photo over before you send it.

Can I use it in Shortcuts?
Yes. The Check Photo action takes a photo and returns a covered copy, for example as part of an automation that checks your latest screenshot.

What is the Control Center button?
Add "Clean Copied Link" to Control Center (swipe down, tap +, then Add a Control). It opens Sanitize ready to paste, so you can clean a link you've just copied in two taps.

Why does Sanitize ask to add photos?
Only when you tap Save to Photos. It can add the clean copy as a new photo, but it can't see your library. Sharing a clean copy works without it.

A link didn't change, or broke.
Sanitize only removes parameters it knows are tracking, and leaves links alone when it isn't sure, so some links come back unchanged on purpose. If a cleaned link stopped working, please send it through TestFlight so the rule can be fixed. You can add your own rules in Settings.

Does Sanitize use the internet?
Only if you tap to look up where a short link (like bit.ly) goes. Everything else, including all link and photo cleaning, happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
