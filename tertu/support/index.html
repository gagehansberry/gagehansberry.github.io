Tertu Support

A private, end-to-end encrypted app for friends, on iPhone and Mac.

Getting help

Email dev.gagehansberry@protonmail.com. Say what you tapped and what you saw; a screenshot helps, but check that it doesn't show messages you'd rather keep private.

Testing through TestFlight? You can also open the TestFlight app, choose Tertu and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Tertu and choose "Share Beta Feedback".

To report someone for abuse, use Report in the app, so the report reaches the developer with the messages you chose. Email can't do that.

Common questions

How do I join?
Tertu is invite-only. Ask a friend who uses it for an invite link, open it on your iPhone or Mac, and follow the steps. You don't need a phone number or an email address.

Can anyone read my messages?
Only the people in the conversation. Messages, photos, calls and everything else you share are end-to-end encrypted on your device, so Tertu's server, and the developer, can't read them. The privacy policy lists what the server does know.

How do I check I'm talking to the right person?
Open the person and choose Verify. Compare the safety number with theirs, ideally in person. If their number later changes, Tertu tells you.

How do I add my Mac or another iPhone?
On the device you already use, open Settings → Devices & Backup and tap Add a Device, then follow the steps on the new one. Your history is copied across, encrypted.

How do I keep my messages if I lose my phone?
Leave Tertu's backup on (Settings → Devices & Backup). It keeps an encrypted copy in your own iCloud, which you can restore when you set up Tertu on a new device signed in to the same Apple Account. A device backup to iCloud or a computer does not include Tertu, and without Tertu's own backup or another linked device, an account can't be recovered: there's no email or phone number to recover it with.

What is membership?
Membership is a one-time purchase in the app of $1.99 in the United States, with no subscription. In other countries the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy. You can also give membership to a friend as a gift, for the same price. Event passes, below, are free.

I bought membership, but it isn't showing.
Open Membership in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Someone sent me an event link and I don't have Tertu.
Open it in your browser to see the invitation and answer with a first name or nickname. Only the hosts can read your answer. If the host accepts you, the page shows the address, and the host may offer you an event pass: a free, temporary Tertu account for the event's chat, which lasts until a week after the event.

How do I report or block someone?
Long-press or right-click a message, or open the person, and choose Report or Block. A report sends the developer the messages it shows you before you send it, never photos, videos or voice messages. Blocking stays on your device.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account. See the privacy policy for what that removes and what remains.
