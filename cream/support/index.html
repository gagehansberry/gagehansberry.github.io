Cream Support

A budgeting app that keeps everything on your iPhone.

Getting help

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with what you tapped and what you saw. A screenshot helps. Please don't send account numbers or other bank details; Cream never needs them.

Testing through TestFlight? You can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in Cream and choose "Share Beta Feedback".

Common questions

How do I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone?
Cream keeps everything on your iPhone and has no account or cloud sync. Use Settings → Export Data to save a file, and Settings → Import Data on the new iPhone to bring it back. Doing this now and then is a good habit, especially while testing.

Why don't I see Quick Add or Ask?
They use Apple Intelligence, which runs on the device and is only on iPhones that support it, with it turned on in Settings. Without it they're hidden, and everything else works the same.

Does Cream connect to my bank?
No. You type in or import what you spend, and Cream never asks for bank logins or account numbers. It can read a screenshot of your bank's app, or a bank alert passed to it by a Shortcuts automation, and put the purchases on a list for you to review.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Erase All Data, or delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.
