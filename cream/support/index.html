Cream Support

A budgeting app that keeps everything on your iPhone or iPad.

Getting help

Email dev.gagehansberry@protonmail.com. Say what you tapped and what you saw; a screenshot helps. Please don't include account numbers or other bank details. Cream never needs them.

Testing through TestFlight? You can also open the TestFlight app, choose Cream and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Cream and choose "Share Beta Feedback".

Common questions

How do I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone or iPad?
Turn on Settings → iCloud Sync on both devices, signed in to the same Apple Account, and Cream keeps them the same. Without sync, Cream keeps everything on your device: use Settings → Export Data to save a file, and Settings → Import Data on the new device to bring it back. Exporting now and then is a good habit either way.

What's in Cream Plus?
Everything except the Transactions tab: budgets and Spending Insights, recurring bills, the calculators, Net Worth, Quick Add and Ask, and importing from screenshots and bank alerts. It's a one-time purchase, shared with your Family Sharing group, and you can try it free for 45 days: Settings → Cream Plus. Without it, adding and changing transactions stays free, everything you entered stays to read and export, and recurring bills pause until you buy it, when any missed ones are added.

I bought Cream Plus, but it shows as locked.
Open Settings → Cream Plus and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Why don't I see Quick Add or Ask?
They use Apple Intelligence, which runs on the device and is only on iPhones and iPads that support it, with it turned on in Settings. Without it they're hidden, and everything else works the same. They're part of Cream Plus.

Does Cream connect to my bank?
No. You type in or import what you spend, and Cream never asks for bank logins or account numbers. It can read a screenshot of your bank's app, a file you download from your bank's website (CSV or OFX: Transactions → ••• → Import Bank File), or a bank alert passed to it by a Shortcuts automation, and put the purchases on a list for you to review.

How do I share a budget?
Turn on the Shared tab in Settings → What Cream Shows, then tap New Shared Budget. To invite someone, open the budget, tap the settings button and choose Invite People; Apple's sharing sheet sends the invitation by Messages, Mail or a link. Only the people you invite can open it; a forwarded link won't work for anyone else. Each person needs an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. When they tap the invitation, Cream opens the budget for them. If they don't have Cream yet, the link takes them to the App Store; after installing it, they tap the invitation once more. You can set each person to Can Edit or View Only, or remove them, from Manage People.

How do I leave a shared budget?
Open it, tap the settings button and choose Leave. You keep your own copy of everything in it up to then, and nothing more is shared between you after that, either way. If you created it, leaving stops sharing it with everyone, and each of them keeps their own copy.

Someone in a shared budget is posting something abusive.
Leave the budget to stop seeing it, or remove them if you created it. Then tell us at dev.gagehansberry@protonmail.com, or use Report a Problem in the budget's settings. We can't see shared budgets, so please describe what happened.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Erase All Data, or delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.
