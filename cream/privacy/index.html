Cream Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Cream does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, which keeps it in your own private iCloud storage, or put it in a shared budget, which shares it with the people you invite through iCloud. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own, except to Apple's App Store for tips and Cream Plus and, if you use them, to iCloud for sync and shared budgets. The developer cannot see anything you put in it.

What Cream keeps on your device

To work, Cream stores what you type into it, in the app's own private storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Transactions: name, amount, date, category and any note you add.
  • Categories, monthly budgets, recurring transactions and savings goals you set up.
  • Net Worth: the nicknames you give accounts and the balances you type. Cream never asks for an account number, login or bank details, and has nowhere to enter them.
  • Numbers typed into the calculators, and your settings, including your answers to the setup questions (such as how often you're paid). Those answers only decide what Cream shows and sets up for you.
  • Shared budgets you make or join (see below).

This storage is protected by iOS data protection, which encrypts it using your device passcode. When Cream is updated, it first keeps a copy of its database on your device (the two most recent), so nothing is lost if an update goes wrong. Those copies never leave the device, are left out of iCloud and computer backups, and are removed by Erase All Data.

Features that use Apple's on-device services

Some features use technology built into iOS. All of it runs on your device.

  • Quick Add, Ask and category suggestions use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence, where available). Your text is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Screenshot import reads the text in a screenshot you paste or choose, using Apple's on-device text recognition. The image is not stored. Choosing one uses the system photo picker, which gives Cream only the picture you pick, not access to your photo library.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. When you use one of Cream's Siri phrases or shortcuts, Siri handles your request under Apple's privacy policy; Cream answers on your device. Text you pass to Cream from a Shortcuts automation, such as a bank alert, goes to a review list. Its wording is deleted as soon as you accept or dismiss it, and the entry itself the next time Cream opens. A pending card hold you haven't reviewed is removed after a week.
  • Bank file import. When you choose a file you downloaded from your bank (CSV or OFX), Cream reads it on your device and keeps only each row's date, amount and description, on a list for you to review; nothing is added until you accept it. The file itself is not stored, and Cream never connects to your bank.
  • Reminders and Your Week. If you turn them on, bill reminders and the weekly "Your Week" note are notifications that iOS schedules and shows on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. A reminder shows the bill's name, and its amount only if you turn on Show Amounts. The weekly note says only "Your Week: See where this week went", with no figures; tapping it works out the summary on your device. Background App Refresh is used only to keep reminders up to date.
  • Spotlight search. If "Show Transactions in Search" is on, the name, amount, category and date of each transaction (never your notes) are added to a search index kept on your device, so Spotlight and Siri can find them. You can turn this off in Cream's Settings, which removes them from the index.

Tips and Cream Plus

Cream offers optional tips, and Cream Plus, a one-time purchase with a free 45-day trial, through Apple's In-App Purchase. When you open the Tip Jar or the Cream Plus screen, buy, start the trial or restore a purchase, your device contacts Apple's App Store to show prices and handle the payment, under Apple's privacy policy. Cream sends nothing you've entered, and keeps no record of your purchases: each time it opens, it asks the App Store on your device whether Cream Plus is active.

iCloud Sync (optional)

Cream can keep your data the same on your iPhone and iPad through iCloud. It's off until you turn it on in Settings → iCloud Sync. When it's on, everything you keep in Cream is stored in your own private iCloud storage, under Apple's privacy policy, and kept in step with your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The bank-alert review list is the exception and never leaves the device. The developer can't see your iCloud data, and names, notes and amounts are also encrypted there. Turning sync off keeps a copy on that device and stops it syncing. To delete Cream's data from iCloud, use Erase All Data in Cream while sync is on, or remove Cream's data in your device's iCloud storage settings.

Shared budgets (optional)

You can keep a budget with people you choose, such as a partner or roommates. Shared budgets use iCloud whether or not iCloud Sync is on, under Apple's privacy policy, and everyone in one needs an Apple Account signed in to iCloud.

  • What is shared. Everything in a shared budget — its name and note, transactions, plans, categories, recurring bills, who added and who paid for each cost, each person's share, and payments recorded between you — is stored in the iCloud account of the person who created it and is visible to everyone in it. Names, notes and amounts are encrypted in iCloud, except the budget's name, which is also the invitation's title and appears on the invitation. The people in a budget are shown by the name their Apple Account provides.
  • What is not. Your own budget, Net Worth, goals and the bank-alert review list are never shared. A transaction from your own budget goes into a shared budget only when you choose, and its note only if you include it. Costs other people add are offered to you as suggestions; nothing is added to your own budget unless you accept it.
  • Invitations are sent with Apple's sharing tools (Messages, Mail or a link), only by the budget's creator, and only to people they choose; a budget can't be joined without one. Cream doesn't access your contacts. The creator can set each person to Can Edit or View Only, and remove anyone.
  • Leaving. Anyone can leave a shared budget at any time and keep their own copy of everything in it up to then; after that nothing more is shared between them. The same happens when the creator removes someone or stops sharing. Nothing is deleted from anyone's device because someone left.
  • Reporting a problem. If someone in a shared budget posts something abusive, use Report a Problem in that budget's settings to contact us, and leave the budget to stop seeing it. Because the developer can't see shared budgets, describe what happened.
  • Cream records payments between people; it never sends or receives money.

Exporting your data

Settings → Export Data creates a file of everything in Cream, but only when you tap it. You choose where that file goes, and what happens to it after that is up to you and the place you save or send it.

What the developer receives

Nothing from the app. Cream sends no data to the developer or to any third party, and contains no third-party code or services. If you leave a tip or buy Cream Plus, Apple processes the payment and gives the developer sales reports that do not identify you.

If you are testing Cream through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That comes from Apple, not from the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

  • Deleting a transaction, a recurring transaction, a goal or a Net Worth account moves it to Settings → Recently Deleted, where you can restore it for 30 days. After that it is removed for good, and you can remove it sooner from the same screen. A deleted recurring transaction is kept while transactions it added are still in your list.
  • Settings → Erase All Data removes everything in Cream: your data, calculator entries, the search index and the pre-update copies of the database. With iCloud Sync on, it also erases Cream's data in iCloud and on your other devices. It also takes you out of every shared budget: one you created is ended in iCloud, one you joined is left. Everyone else in them keeps their own copy.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, including its search index. A Cream Plus purchase stays with your Apple Account; Restore Purchases brings it back.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Cream's data in that backup, under your device's backup settings and Apple's terms.

Children

Cream is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Cream ever begins sending data off your device, this policy will say exactly what, to whom and why before that version of the app is released.
