Cream Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Cream does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own; tips and Cream Plus are handled by Apple's App Store. The developer cannot see anything you put in it.

What Cream keeps on your device

To work, Cream stores what you type into it, in the app's own private storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Transactions: name, amount, date, category and any note you add.
  • Categories, monthly budgets and recurring transactions you set up.
  • Net Worth: the nicknames you give accounts and the balances you type. Cream never asks for an account number, login or bank details, and has nowhere to enter them.
  • Numbers typed into the calculators, and your settings.

This storage is protected by iOS data protection, which encrypts it using your device passcode. When Cream is updated, it first keeps a copy of its database on your device (the two most recent), so nothing is lost if an update goes wrong. Those copies never leave the device, are left out of iCloud and computer backups, and are removed by Erase All Data.

Features that use Apple's on-device services

Some features use technology built into iOS. All of it runs on your device.

  • Quick Add, Ask and category suggestions use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence, where available). Your text is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Screenshot import reads the text in a screenshot you paste or choose, using Apple's on-device text recognition. The image is not stored. Choosing one uses the system photo picker, which gives Cream only the picture you pick, not access to your photo library.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. When you use one of Cream's Siri phrases or shortcuts, Siri handles your request under Apple's privacy policy; Cream answers on your device. Text you pass to Cream from a Shortcuts automation, such as a bank alert, goes to a review list. Its wording is deleted as soon as you accept or dismiss it, and the entry itself the next time Cream opens. A pending card hold you haven't reviewed is removed after a week.
  • Spotlight search. If "Show Transactions in Search" is on, the name, amount, category and date of each transaction (never your notes) are added to a search index kept on your device, so Spotlight and Siri can find them. You can turn this off in Cream's Settings, which removes them from the index.

Tips and Cream Plus

Cream offers optional tips, and Cream Plus, a one-time purchase with a free 45-day trial, through Apple's In-App Purchase. When you open the Tip Jar or the Cream Plus screen, buy, start the trial or restore a purchase, your device contacts Apple's App Store to show prices and handle the payment, under Apple's privacy policy. Cream sends nothing you've entered, and keeps no record of your purchases: each time it opens, it asks the App Store on your device whether Cream Plus is active.

Exporting your data

Settings → Export Data creates a file of everything in Cream, but only when you tap it. You choose where that file goes, and what happens to it after that is up to you and the place you save or send it.

What the developer receives

Nothing from the app. Cream sends no data to the developer or to any third party, and contains no third-party code or services. If you leave a tip or buy Cream Plus, Apple processes the payment and gives the developer sales reports that do not identify you.

If you are testing Cream through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That comes from Apple, not from the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

  • Deleting a transaction, a recurring transaction or a Net Worth account moves it to Settings → Recently Deleted, where you can restore it for 30 days. After that it is removed for good, and you can remove it sooner from the same screen. A deleted recurring transaction is kept while transactions it added are still in your list.
  • Settings → Erase All Data removes everything in Cream: your data, calculator entries, the search index and the pre-update copies of the database.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, including its search index. A Cream Plus purchase stays with your Apple Account; Restore Purchases brings it back.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Cream's data in that backup, under your device's backup settings and Apple's terms.

Children

Cream is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Cream ever begins sending data off your device, this policy will say exactly what, to whom and why before that version of the app is released.
