Questions

Does Cream connect to my bank?

No. You type in or import what you spend, and Cream never asks for a bank login or account number. It can read a screenshot of your bank's app and put the purchases on a list for you to check first.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Adding and changing transactions stays free, and everything you entered stays to read and export. Budgets show where things stood the day the trial ended, and recurring bills pause until you buy Plus, when any missed ones are added.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. With iCloud sync on, both stay the same.

Is this financial advice?

No. Cream does arithmetic on the numbers you give it. It doesn't recommend products, move money, or tell you what to do.
